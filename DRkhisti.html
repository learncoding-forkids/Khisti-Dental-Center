DR. KHISTI

Dr. Khisti came to USA in 2003 after getting married to Rahul Khisti, RPh, PhD. and now she has a beautiful daughter named Somya and adorable boy named Saleel. She has been serving patients in the Richmond Metropolitan area for over 15 years.

Dr. Khisti strives to further her education and offers her patients the best dental care services through extensive knowledge and state-of-the-art equipment. She spends over 100 hours each year in continuing education courses so she can provide her patients with the most up-to-date procedures. She enjoys spending time with her family and friends, cooking, and playing tennis.

Whether you come to Dr. Khisti for cosmetic dentistry, pediatric Dentistry, restorative dentistry or dental implants, she and her skilled team will offer you the best care in a truly friendly environment.

Dr. Khisti received her Bachelor of Dentistry (BDS) from Government Dental College, Nagpur India in 2001. She then went on to receive her Doctorate in Dental Medicine (DMD) with honors from the Goldman School of Dental Medicine, Boston University, MA in 2008.

Dr. Khisti is a member of many dental and community organizations including: American Dental Association (ADA) Virginia Dental Association (VDA)
